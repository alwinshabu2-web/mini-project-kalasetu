About KalaSetu

Where Classical Dance Finds Its Voice

KalaSetu — meaning "Bridge to Art" — is a sanctuary dedicated entirely to the classical dance traditions of India. Our master Gurus carry lineages from Kalakshetra, Gandharva Mahavidyalaya and Sangeet Natak Akademi, bringing authentic pedagogy to every student from the very first ta-dhi-gi-na.

We offer examination-graded courses in six major classical dance forms — each taught with rigorous attention to abhinaya, nritta, and natya — structured so students can compete nationally and perform professionally.
